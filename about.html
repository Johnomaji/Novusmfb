Our story

Built in Benue, for Benue

Novus Microfinance Bank was licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria on 8 March 2024 to serve the banking needs of the people of Benue State.

We exist to support individuals, nano and small businesses, farmers and cooperatives with low-cost, high-impact financial products designed around how people here actually live and work: market days, planting seasons, school terms and all.

In our first two years we disbursed more than ₦6.35 billion in loans to over 11,000 people and businesses, and we are only getting started.
